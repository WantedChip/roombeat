# Support & Community Resources

Thank you for using RoomBeat! We want you to have the best possible experience synchronizing audio across your devices.

Here are the best ways to get support, report issues, and connect with the project:

---

## 💬 Community Discussions & Questions

If you have questions about:
- Supported hardware setups, Wi-Fi routers, or portable hotspots
- Calibration tips for specific smartphone speaker configurations
- General inquiries on how RoomBeat achieves sub-10ms acoustic synchronization

Please participate in **[GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/WantedChip/RoomBeat/discussions)** rather than opening an issue.

---

## 🐛 Bug Reports & Technical Defects

If you encounter a reproducible bug, audio stutter, crash, or unexpected behavior:
1. Search existing **[GitHub Issues](https://github.com/WantedChip/RoomBeat/issues)** to see if the issue has already been reported.
2. If not, open a new report using our **[Bug Report Template](https://github.com/WantedChip/RoomBeat/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml)**.
3. Be sure to include your device model, Android OS version, and network configuration.

---

## 💡 Feature Requests & Architectural Ideas

If you have an idea for an enhancement:
- Open a proposal using our **[Feature Request Template](https://github.com/WantedChip/RoomBeat/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml)**.
- Ensure the proposal respects RoomBeat's core principles: zero cloud reliance, 100% local network operation, and low-latency audio.

---

## 🔒 Security & Privacy Inquiries

If you suspect a security vulnerability or privacy concern:
- **Do not open a public issue.**
- Review our **[Security Policy](./SECURITY.md)** and email **ty121rt@gmail.com** directly for private triage and resolution.
