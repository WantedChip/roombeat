RoomBeat Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 27, 2026 • Version 1.0.0 • WantedChip Open Source

Core Commitment: Zero Telemetry, 100% Offline Local Network

RoomBeat is designed from the silicon up to be an air-gapped, local-only acoustic system. We do not collect, transmit, store, sell, or analyze any personal information, audio streams, device identifiers, or telemetry. All communication happens directly between phones on your local Wi-Fi or portable hotspot.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

RoomBeat adheres strictly to a zero-knowledge, zero-collection operational paradigm:

  • No Personal Identifiers: We do not ask for or collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, usernames, or contact lists.
  • No Device or Hardware IDs: We do not log Android IDs, IMEI numbers, MAC addresses, advertising IDs (GAID), or hardware serials.
  • No Location Data: RoomBeat never requests or accesses fine or coarse GPS/network location permissions.
  • No Telemetry or Usage Analytics: There are no third-party trackers (e.g., Google Analytics, Firebase, Sentry, Mixpanel, AppsFlyer) embedded in RoomBeat.
  • No Cloud Storage or Remote Backends: RoomBeat has no central database, remote server, or user authentication cloud. Your phone is the entire system.

2. Local Network Architecture & Traffic Isolation

All network communication initiated by RoomBeat is strictly isolated to your local Area Network (LAN) or portable Wi-Fi hotspot:

  • UDP Multicast (Port 4242): Audio packets encoded in 20ms Opus frames are broadcast locally across the multicast group 239.255.42.99:4242. Multicast packets never cross the default internet gateway router.
  • Local Socket Control Plane (Port 8080): The host device hosts an embedded TCP socket server to manage room lobbies and clock calibration. Only authorized local peers on the same subnet can connect.
  • mDNS / Zero-Configuration Discovery: Service discovery uses standard Android Network Service Discovery (_roombeat._tcp) broadcast exclusively on your local subnet.

3. Audio Processing & Ingest Pipelines

RoomBeat handles audio exclusively in volatile RAM on the local device:

  • System App Audio Capture (Android 11+ / API 30+): System audio capture uses Android's official AudioPlaybackCaptureConfiguration paired with a foreground service (type mediaProjection). Audio data is read directly into memory, encoded to Opus, and transmitted over local UDP multicast. Audio is never written to persistent disk storage, recorded to files, or sent to external servers. Every capture session requires explicit confirmation via Android's system consent dialog.
  • Local Storage Audio Playback: When selecting local audio files, RoomBeat uses the Android Storage Access Framework (SAF) and scoped READ_MEDIA_AUDIO permission. Audio is decoded directly from local storage into memory. No file contents or file metadata leave your local device and room network.
  • Spotify App Remote Synchronization: When using Spotify mode, RoomBeat uses the official Spotify Android App Remote SDK. Playback commands (track URI, seek timestamp) are communicated directly to the Spotify application installed on your device via Android IPC. RoomBeat does not intercept, collect, or store your Spotify credentials, authentication tokens, or playback history.

4. Camera & QR Code Scanning

RoomBeat includes an optional QR code scanner to simplify joining rooms. Camera video frames are processed entirely on-device in volatile memory by the Google MLKit / ZXing barcode scanning engine. No camera images, photographs, or video frames are ever captured, saved to storage, or transmitted over any network. Camera access ceases immediately when the QR scanner sheet is closed.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

RoomBeat does not collect, maintain, or process personal data from any person, including children under the age of 13. Because RoomBeat operates strictly offline on local Wi-Fi without user registration or cloud servers, it is safe for all audiences.
